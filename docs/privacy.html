Synclone privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Synclone is an open-source desktop app that runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own.

What Synclone accesses

When you connect a cloud storage account, such as Google Drive, Synclone accesses the files, folders and their details (names, sizes, dates) in that account. It uses this access only to do what the app is for: showing your files on your computer, downloading the ones you open or choose to keep, uploading the ones you add or change, and moving or deleting files when you ask it to.

Where your data goes

  • Your files travel directly between your computer and your cloud storage provider.
  • Your sign-in tokens, a list of your files, and any files you download are stored on your own computer.
  • Synclone does not send your files, file details, sign-in tokens or usage information to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Synclone contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Google user data

Synclone's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google Drive is used only to provide the features described above, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by any person other than you.

Removing access

You can disconnect a cloud storage account in Synclone at any time, which removes its sign-in from your computer. You can also revoke Synclone's access to a Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting does not delete anything from your cloud storage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be raised at github.com/seantoler/Synclone/issues.
