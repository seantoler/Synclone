Synclone

A free, open-source desktop app that shows your cloud storage as ordinary folders on Windows and macOS.

What it does

  • Shows every file in your cloud storage as a placeholder that takes no disk space.
  • Downloads a file when you open it, and keeps it until you choose to remove it from the device.
  • Uploads files you add or change.
  • Holds deletions in a queue: nothing is deleted from your cloud storage until you confirm it.

Status

Synclone is in early development and is not yet released.

Works with many cloud services

Synclone connects to Google Drive, OneDrive, Drime and the many other storage services that rclone supports. Each one appears as its own folder inside your Synclone folder.

How it uses your cloud accounts

When you connect an account, Synclone asks that service for access so it can list, download, upload, move and delete your files at your direction. This applies to every service, including Google Drive. Synclone runs entirely on your own computer and talks to each service directly. See the privacy policy.
